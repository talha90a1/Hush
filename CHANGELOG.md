# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Automatic updates through Sparkle, and a DMG download.
- One volume control: Hush's volume over a safe speaker level, with the monitor's own speakers raised only at the top of the scale. The safe level is restored on quit.
- Night dimming from sunset to sunrise or fixed hours, remembering day and night brightness.
- Contrast, speaker level and input switching under More Controls.
- Dimming below the monitor's lowest backlight level.
- Several monitors, with the brightness keys acting on the screen under the pointer.
- Control Center–style sliders and a Liquid Glass volume and brightness pop-up.
- The volume is restored after a restart instead of jumping to 100%.
- Settings to turn each of these off.

## 1.0.0

- First release: an English fork of Mando.
